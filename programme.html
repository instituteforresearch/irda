PhD & Doctoral Research Support Programme

The IRDA PhD and Doctoral Research Support Programme offers an interdisciplinary approach to examining social, economic, medical, and political issues within the context of developing countries through the lens of modern data science. The programme draws on theories and methodologies from a range of disciplines, including data science, statistics, economics, sociology, political science, and geography, to promote a comprehensive understanding of development processes and outcomes.

The curriculum is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in development theory, advanced research methods, machine learning, and relevant disciplinary perspectives through rigorous coursework. A central component of the programme is the completion of an original research project, usually in the form of a thesis, through which students demonstrate their capacity for independent, critical, and high-quality scholarly inquiry that leverages data to address real-world development challenges.

Students may also choose to complete their thesis through a chapter-by-publication format, where each study objective is developed into a publishable paper before the submission of the final thesis. This option is subject to formal application and approval by the School of Graduate Studies.

Graduates of the programme are well prepared for careers in academia, research institutions, government agencies, non-governmental organisations, and international organisations where data-informed decision-making is central to development practice.

The PhD and Doctoral Research Support Programme runs for one year (two semesters), combining intensive foundational coursework in semester one with focused, supervised thesis research in semester two.

Semester one covers core modules in development theory, statistical inference, machine learning for social science, data ethics, and research design. In semester two, students focus on specialised electives, field data collection, and the completion of their doctoral thesis under the supervision of a faculty committee.

Applicants must satisfy the following requirements:

  • A first degree with at least Second Class Lower Division. Applicants with a Third Class may also be considered if they have a minimum of five (5) years of relevant working experience.
  • A relevant Master's degree (MA, MSc, or MPhil) in Data Science, Statistics, the Social Sciences, or Humanities from a recognised or accredited university.
  • Demonstrable quantitative or computational aptitude, evidenced through prior coursework, professional experience, or a submitted data portfolio.
  • Submission of relevant supporting documents, including certificates, transcripts, curriculum vitae, a research proposal, and personal background information.

Please note: Shortlisted applicants will be invited for an interview.

Applicants should contact IRDA directly or visit our office for full details on the application process, including completion and submission of the application form, upload of supporting documents, and payment of the application fee.

Master's in Research Analysis

The Master's in Research Analysis is a practical, career-focused graduate programme designed for students, professionals, and organisations who want hands-on mastery of modern research and data analysis techniques.

Over two intensive semesters, participants build a strong foundation in research design and statistical methods before applying that knowledge to a supervised capstone research project. Teaching blends lectures, software labs, and real datasets so that every module produces a practical, usable skill.

Graduates leave the programme able to design a study, collect and clean data, run appropriate statistical or qualitative analysis, and communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences alike.

Courses & Modules

01. Foundations of Research Methods

Core principles of research design, hypothesis formulation, and the research process from idea to write-up.

02. Quantitative Data Analysis (SPSS & STATA)

Hands-on training in descriptive and inferential statistics using industry-standard statistical software.

03. Qualitative Research & Thematic Analysis

Interview and focus-group techniques, coding, and thematic analysis for non-numeric data.

04. Survey Design & Sampling Techniques

Designing valid questionnaires and choosing appropriate sampling strategies for reliable results.

05. Applied Biostatistics & Epidemiological Methods

Statistical methods commonly used in health, clinical, and biological research.

06. Data Visualisation & Reporting

Turning analysis into clear charts, dashboards, and reports that communicate findings effectively.

07. Research Ethics & Proposal Writing

Ethical considerations in research and how to write a compelling, fundable research proposal.

08. Capstone Research Project

A supervised, independent research project applying the skills learned across the programme.

Semester 1 covers the core foundations: research methods, quantitative analysis, and data management. Semester 2 builds on this with applied and qualitative modules, culminating in a supervised capstone research project presented at the end of the programme.

Open to everyone. There are no eligibility restrictions — anyone can apply.

Open Enrolment — No Restrictions:

  • This programme is open to everyone. There is no restriction on academic background, field of study, age, or professional experience.
  • Prior research or statistics experience is helpful but not required — the programme is designed to build these skills from the ground up.
  • Students, working professionals, and career changers are all welcome to apply.
  • Submission of a completed application form and a valid form of identification.

Anyone with an interest in research and data analysis may apply — there are no eligibility restrictions.

Applicants should contact IRDA directly or visit our office to complete the application form, submit their identification, and pay the applicable programme fee. Classes for new intakes begin at the start of each semester.

Diploma in Research Analysis

The Diploma in Research Analysis is a six-month introductory programme built for anyone who wants a fast, practical entry point into research and data analysis — no previous experience necessary.

Across two terms, participants are introduced to the fundamentals of research design, basic statistics, and everyday data-analysis tools, before applying what they've learned in a short practical project or fieldwork attachment.

The diploma is well suited to recent school leavers, undergraduates, and working professionals looking to add practical research skills to their CV in a short amount of time.

Courses & Modules

01. Introduction to Research Analysis

A beginner-friendly introduction to what research analysis is and why it matters.

02. Basic Statistics for Research

Core statistical concepts explained simply, with no prior maths background required.

03. Data Collection Tools & Techniques

Practical methods for gathering reliable data, from surveys to interviews.

04. Introduction to SPSS / Excel for Data Analysis

Hands-on practice analysing data using everyday, widely available software tools.

05. Report Writing & Presentation Skills

How to turn findings into a clear written report and confident presentation.

06. Practical Project / Fieldwork Attachment

A short, guided practical project applying everything learned during the diploma.

Term 1 introduces the fundamentals: research analysis basics, statistics, and data collection tools. Term 2 focuses on applied software skills, reporting, and a short practical project or fieldwork attachment.

Open to everyone. There are no eligibility restrictions — anyone can apply.

Open Enrolment — No Restrictions:

  • This programme is open to anyone. There is no restriction on academic background, age, or work experience.
  • No prior research or statistics knowledge is required — the diploma starts from the basics.
  • Recent school leavers, undergraduates, and working professionals are all welcome to apply.
  • Submission of a completed application form and a valid form of identification.

Anyone with an interest in research and data analysis may apply — there are no eligibility restrictions.

Applicants should contact IRDA directly or visit our office to complete the application form, submit their identification, and pay the applicable programme fee. New intakes begin at the start of each term.
