Data Analysis (SPSS, STATA, EVIEWS)

SPSS, STATA, EVIEWS, R, Python

Expert statistical analysis using SPSS, STATA, EVIEWS, R, and Python. We handle complex datasets, econometric modelling, and interpretation for medical, biological, and social science research.

Expertise in descriptive and inferential statistics, regression analysis, survival analysis, panel data modelling, time-series econometrics, and machine learning pipelines. We deliver clean datasets, publication-ready tables and figures, and a written interpretation of every result.

What's included

  • Descriptive statistics
  • Inferential tests (t-test, ANOVA, chi-square)
  • Regression & panel data models
  • Time-series & forecasting
  • Survival analysis
  • Data visualisation
